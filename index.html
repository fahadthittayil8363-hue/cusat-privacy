Newton Kuttan – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Newton Kuttan is a study app with past exam questions and model papers. It is published by fvfahad ("we", "us"). This page explains what the app does with information. It is not affiliated with CUSAT.

What stays on your device

The app has no sign-up and no account. These things are saved on your own device only, and our code never sends them anywhere:

  • Your answer to "Are you 18 or older?" (saved as one word: adult or under 18).
  • Your progress: your answers, bookmarks and the numbers worked out from them.
  • A test in progress or just finished, including its timer.
  • Your theme choice (light or dark).
  • Which chapters or papers you have unlocked by watching an ad, and when each unlock ends (24 hours).

We do not run our own server to collect this information, so we cannot see it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

Ads

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner ad, and an optional "watch a short ad to unlock for 24 hours" ad that only starts when you tap the button. When ads are shown, Google's ad software runs in the app and Google may collect and use information from your device to show and measure ads. We do not control this. Please read how Google handles it: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

No ads are shown in the web version of the app.

Children and people under 18

The first time you open the app, it asks whether you are 18 or older. The app uses your answer like this:

  • Under 18: the app tells Google the user is under the age of consent and asks for child-directed treatment. Ads are limited to Google's "G" content rating, ads are not personalised, and no ad consent form is shown.
  • 18 or older: ads are limited to Google's "PG" content rating. Where Google says it is required, a consent form is shown. Ads are personalised only if you agree in that form. Otherwise they are not personalised. You can change your choice later in Settings when this option is available.

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, or any other personal details in the app. If you are under 18, we recommend asking a parent or guardian if you have questions about ads.

Changing or deleting your information

Because the information above is stored only on your device, you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. For ad information held by Google, use Google's own controls described on the page linked above.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this page, we will update it and change the date at the top.
